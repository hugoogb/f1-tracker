world.geo.json
==============

Country outlines derived from Natural Earth (1:110m Admin 0 - Countries).

Source:  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
Licence: PUBLIC DOMAIN -- no attribution required, no commercial restriction.
         https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Changes: non-geometry properties stripped (only `name` kept) and coordinates
rounded to 2 decimal places to reduce transfer size.

This replaced the previous CARTO raster basemap, which required visible
CARTO + OpenStreetMap attribution and was restricted to non-commercial use
under its free tier.
